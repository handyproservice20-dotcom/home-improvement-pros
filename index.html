Handy Pro Services was founded in 2010 with a simple belief: if you do quality work and treat people right, the business takes care of itself. For over 15 years, that philosophy has driven every project we've completed across Hunterdon County.

We're not a large franchise. We're a family-owned business where the same people who answer your call are the ones showing up to your home. That means accountability, pride in the work, and a reputation we protect on every job site.

From small painting jobs to full kitchen and bathroom renovations, we bring the same level of care to every project — because for our customers, their home is their biggest investment.
